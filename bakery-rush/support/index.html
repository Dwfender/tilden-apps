How to play

  • Merge: drag a treat onto a matching treat to bake the next one up: Crumb → Cookie → Cupcake → Donut → Croissant → Pie → Cake. Two Cakes throw a party for a big bonus.
  • Serve: customers show the treat they want. Drag that treat onto them. Serve quickly to earn a tip.
  • Combos: make merges or serves within 2 seconds of each other to build a combo, up to 4× points.
  • Game over: new treats keep landing on the counter, faster as you go. When the counter is full, the game ends.

Common questions

What's the difference between Daily Rush and Endless?

Daily Rush is the same bakery for every player that day, and you get one try. It resets at midnight your local time. Endless is a fresh bakery every game, so you can practise as much as you like and chase your best score.

I missed a day. Did I lose my streak?

Yes. The streak counts days in a row on which you played the Daily Rush.

Can I play the Daily Rush again?

No. You get one try per day, so everyone's score is comparable. Endless is unlimited.

How do I turn off sound?

During a game, tap the pause button (top left) and switch off Sound.

Will my scores move to a new phone?

Not at the moment. Scores are saved only on the phone you play on.

Does Bakery Rush have ads or in-app purchases?

No.

Does the game collect my data?

No. See the Bakery Rush Privacy Policy.
