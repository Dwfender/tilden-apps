Bakery Rush Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

The short version. Bakery Rush is a puzzle game for iPhone. It does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. Everything it saves stays on your phone.

Who we are

Bakery Rush is published by Tilden Ops, LLC, based in New York, United States. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Tilden Ops, LLC. "You" means the person playing Bakery Rush.

Questions about privacy: dan@tildenops.com

What the game saves on your phone

To remember your progress, Bakery Rush saves a small amount of game information on your device:

  • Your best Endless score
  • Your Daily Rush results (score, customers served, cakes made, time played) and your daily streak
  • Whether you have seen the how-to-play screens
  • Whether sound is on or off

This information stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. We cannot see it. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not collect

Bakery Rush does not:

  • Ask for your name, email address or any account
  • Collect your location, contacts, photos or any other personal data
  • Use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools
  • Use your device's advertising identifier or track you across apps or websites
  • Ask for any device permissions
  • Contain in-app purchases
  • Connect to the internet or to any server

Sharing your score

After a game you can tap Share to send a short text summary of your score. This opens the standard iPhone share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it, and it goes directly to the app or person you pick, not to us.

Children

Bakery Rush is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your saved game data lives only on your phone, and you can remove it at any time by deleting the app. If you have a question or request under the GDPR, CCPA or any other privacy law, contact us and we will answer within thirty days.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Bakery Rush changes how it handles data (for example, by adding ads or optional purchases), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.
